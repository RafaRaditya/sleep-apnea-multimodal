To operationalize Experiment 4, the harmonization 
comparison will involve the following training and 
evaluation configurations, subject to verification of signal 
availability and annotation compatibility during the dataset 
audit: (i) single-dataset training evaluated on held-out 
subjects from the same dataset; (ii) combined-dataset training 
evaluated on held-out subjects across all datasets; and, where 
dataset compatibility permits, (iii) leave-one-dataset-out 
evaluation, in which one dataset is entirely excluded from 
training and used only for testing, following the logic of [27] 
and [23]. 
 
In terms of variables, the independent variables are the model 
configuration (proposed, ECG-only, respiratory-only, simple 
fusion), window duration (10 s vs. 30 s), and preprocessing 

setting. The dependent variables are accuracy, sensitivity, 
specificity, precision, F1-score, and the area under the 
receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC). Controlled 
variables are the subject-level data partitions, optimizer, 
training budget, batch size, learning-rate tuning rule, labeling 
strategy, evaluation protocol, and random seeds, which are 
held constant across comparisons for a fair assessment. 
Through this design, the study aims to provide an empirical 
evaluation of the combined effects of harmonization, 
preprocessing, temporal window reduction, and cross-
attention fusion on sleep apnea detection.
